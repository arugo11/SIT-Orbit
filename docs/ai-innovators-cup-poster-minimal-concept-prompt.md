Style/medium: serious contemporary academic conference poster, Swiss-influenced editorial typography, abundant white space, strict grid, no marketing-poster gloss.

3. Bottom 23%: three equal text columns without boxes or tinted backgrounds. Headings: "AI", "CODE", "HUMAN". Under them respectively: "意図理解・根拠整理・一手生成", "データ境界・根拠検証・書込み停止", "承認・変更・却下". Typography: clean Japanese sans-serif, bold declarative headings, body at a visibly readable academic-poster scale, left aligned, consistent baseline grid. The thesis line and the central schematic dominate.
Constraints: keep at least 35% of the poster as white or breathing space; no gradients; no shadows; no rounded app cards; no decorative orbit rings; no stars; no mascots; no photos; no device mockups; no fake QR code; no extra logos; no excessive icons; no multicolor infographic; no prose paragraphs; no invented success badges; no tiny unreadable text; no additional labels beyond those requested. This is an academic poster, not a startup advertisement.
